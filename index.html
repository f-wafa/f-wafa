01 — about me

Clean UI, thoughtful code, and a soft spot for cats.

I'm Faiz — a student from Indonesia who fell in love with the web. What started as curiosity about how websites work turned into a daily habit of building, breaking, and rebuilding interfaces.

I focus on the frontend fundamentals: semantic HTML, modern CSS, JavaScript/TypeScript, and component-driven UI with React and Next.js. I care about how a page feels — fast, accessible, and pleasant to use.

Right now I'm leveling up my understanding of web architecture and design systems, one small project at a time. I learn in public, ask a lot of questions, and keep shipping.
